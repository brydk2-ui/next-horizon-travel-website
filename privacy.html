Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

Next Horizon Travel Co. respects your privacy. This policy explains the information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information You Provide

When you contact us or submit the Plan My Vacation form, you may provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, travel dates, destinations, traveler information, budget, accommodation preferences and other trip details you choose to share.

How We Use Information

We use information you submit to respond to your inquiry, communicate with you about travel planning, prepare or discuss travel options, and provide the services you request. We may also use basic technical information to maintain, secure and improve the website.

Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information submitted through this website. When you choose to move forward with travel planning or booking, information may need to be provided to travel suppliers or service providers as necessary to research, arrange or service your trip. Their own privacy practices may apply.

Website Analytics and Third-Party Services

This website may use analytics, advertising or social-media technologies to understand site usage and measure marketing. If these services are enabled, they may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. We will update this policy as our website tools change.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information submitted to us. No website or email system can guarantee absolute security. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for travel planning, business records, legal obligations and legitimate business purposes.

Your Choices

You may choose not to submit the online form and instead contact us by phone or email. You may also contact us to ask questions about information you previously submitted through this website.

Children

This website is intended for adults arranging travel. Please do not have a child submit personal information directly through the website.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to bryan@nexthorizontravelco.com or by calling 800-721-9305.

This website privacy notice describes Next Horizon Travel Co.'s current website practices and is not intended as legal advice.
